The Mac is the main writer and syncs only while it is awake. Before stating "nothing
happened", "X is overdue", or "no applications/prep this week", check freshness:
- the newest **non-`hermes:`** log header (`grep -rh '^## \[' Log/ | grep -v '| hermes:' | sort | tail -1`);
  your own entries prove nothing about whether his writes are arriving;

the vault may be behind (last update from the Mac: <date>)"** — never report the absence
as a fact.
